Consent & Data Notice

ARS — Adarsh Ke Alfaz

1. Purpose

This notice explains the types of information that may be requested when a user registers for an ARS service and the purposes for which that information may be used.

2. Information That May Be Requested

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Registered mobile number
  • Class or educational information
  • Exam registration details
  • Join ARS application information
  • Information needed for certificate generation

3. Why Information Is Needed

Information may be needed for account creation, identity/contact verification, exam registration, examination administration, results, certificates, Join ARS applications, notifications and support.

4. Examination-Related Data

Where applicable, an online examination system may process answers, marks, timing information and system events necessary to operate and evaluate the examination.

Any examination integrity or monitoring feature should be separately described in the relevant examination instructions and implemented according to applicable requirements.

5. Student and Minor Users

ARS may serve school students. Where applicable, users who are minors should involve a parent or guardian and follow any required consent process before submitting personal information.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

ARS may use service providers for authentication, database hosting, storage, email, analytics or certificate-related functionality. Information should be shared only to the extent reasonably necessary for the relevant service and applicable requirements.

7. Public Verification

Certificate verification may make limited certificate information available through an official verification page. The public verification system should avoid exposing unnecessary personal information.

8. Security

Authentication credentials and sensitive records should be managed through a secure backend. Passwords, private credentials and secret API keys must not be embedded in public website source code.

9. User Requests

Users may contact ARS about applicable questions relating to their submitted information, subject to identity verification and applicable requirements.

10. Consent Confirmation

Where a registration or service requires an explicit consent confirmation, the user should read the applicable notice before continuing.

11. Withdrawal or Change of Preference

Depending on the service and legal requirements, users may be able to withdraw or change certain permissions or preferences by contacting ARS. Withdrawal may not affect information that must be retained for a legitimate operational, legal, security or record-keeping purpose.

12. Related Policies

Please also review the ARS Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions and Examination Rules where applicable.
