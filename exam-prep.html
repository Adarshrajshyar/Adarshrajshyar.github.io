Starter revision notes

Core skills to practise

Mathematics

Remember: show your work, include units and check whether the answer is reasonable.

  • Fractions and decimals
  • Factors, multiples, HCF and LCM
  • Ratio, percentage and averages
  • Measurement, area and perimeter
  • Word problems and mental calculation

Mental Ability

Look for one rule at a time and eliminate choices that break the rule.

  • Number and figure patterns
  • Classification and analogy
  • Direction and order questions
  • Simple coding-decoding
  • Visual rotation and completion

Language & GK

Read the full question carefully before choosing an answer.

  • Reading comprehension
  • Vocabulary and grammar
  • Hindi/English practice as required
  • Science, India, Bihar and environment
  • Verify current affairs by date
