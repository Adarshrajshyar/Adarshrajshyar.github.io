The Journey of Life

Every road has turns unknown,
Every seed has time to be grown.
Take one step when skies are gray,
Small steps can shape a brighter day.

A Little Hope

When the night feels long and wide,
Keep a little hope inside.
Dawn may come beyond your view,
And bring a sky of brighter blue.

Whispers of Nature

The leaves move softly in the breeze,
The clouds sail slowly past the trees.
The earth has stories, calm and deep,
In every flower, a dream to keep.

Dreams Take Flight

A dream begins with one small spark,
A steady heart can light the dark.
Learn, keep trying, rise once more,
Each new day opens another door.
