Grievance & Support

Submit a concern, complaint, technical issue or service-related request to ARS.

1. Purpose

ARS provides this channel for users who need to report service issues, certificate concerns, examination problems, account issues, content concerns or other legitimate grievances.

2. Common Support Categories

Exam Registration, attempt or technical concerns
Certificate Verification or record-related concerns
Account Login and profile-related issues

3. Recommended Complaint Information

To help ARS investigate a complaint, include accurate information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Relevant registration or application ID
  • Exam or certificate information where applicable
  • Date and approximate time of the issue
  • A clear description of the problem
  • Relevant supporting information where appropriate

4. Review Process

  1. The grievance is received through the available ARS support channel.
  2. Relevant details are reviewed.
  3. Additional information may be requested where necessary.
  4. The issue is assigned to an authorized person or department where applicable.
  5. The outcome or next step is communicated through the available contact method.
Important examination, result and certificate decisions should be handled through the applicable authorized workflow and should be recorded where an audit record is required.

5. Submit a Grievance

6. Important Note

Do not submit passwords, OTPs, payment card details, private authentication credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information through a grievance form.

For urgent account-security concerns, use the official ARS support channel as soon as possible.

7. Contact

General enquiries can also be submitted through the official ARS Contact page.
