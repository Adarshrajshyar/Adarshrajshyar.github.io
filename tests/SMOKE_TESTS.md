# Manual smoke-test checklist

- [ ] Open `index.html` and confirm logo/banner render.
- [ ] At desktop width, open every public nav link.
- [ ] At mobile width, open and close the navigation menu.
- [ ] Switch light/dark theme and reload.
- [ ] Search for `RMS`, `Shayari`, `Certificate` and confirm results.
- [ ] Switch categories on Shayari, Stories and Poetry pages.
- [ ] Answer the sample MCQ on `exams.html`.
- [ ] Submit the preview-only Join ARS and Contact forms; confirm they say no data was saved/sent.
- [ ] Open both book pages and the two markdown manuscript files.
- [ ] Open Private, Admin preview and Publisher preview; ensure their demo-only warning is visible.
- [ ] Confirm no fake sponsor names, visitor figures or official-affiliation claims appear.
- [ ] Before launch, run authenticated and unauthenticated backend permission tests; do not skip these.
