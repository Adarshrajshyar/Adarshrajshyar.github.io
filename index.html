Start your preparation

Small, steady steps count.

Use the plan to practise regularly. Always check each entrance exam’s latest official admission notice for eligibility, dates and syllabus.

Plan

Study a little each day

Review one concept, solve a few questions, and note mistakes for the weekend.

Revise

Keep an error notebook

Write the question type, the correction and the idea you want to remember.

Review

Check your progress

Use practice sets for learning. Do not treat sample questions as official papers.
