ARS Official Policy

Guidelines for using the ARS Official website and its digital features.

1. About ARS Official

ARS Official is a digital platform containing creative content, information, digital tools, certificates, verification services and other platform features.

2. Content Policy

  • Users should submit genuine and appropriate content.
  • Do not submit content that violates applicable laws.
  • Do not impersonate another person or organization.
  • Respect copyright and intellectual property rights.
  • Do not intentionally submit misleading information.

3. Certificate Policy

Certificates generated through ARS Official should be treated as digital records generated by the platform. Verification should always be performed through the official verification page using the certificate ID.

Certificate information must not be intentionally altered, misrepresented or used for fraudulent purposes.

4. Certificate Verification

The verification system is designed to accept supported ARS certificate IDs, including applicable joining certificate IDs. The verification result depends on the records available to the verification system.

5. Publisher Policy

  • Publish only content you have the right to use.
  • Do not intentionally upload false information.
  • Respect other creators and contributors.
  • ARS Official may review or remove inappropriate content.

6. Privacy

Some website features may use browser storage such as localStorage to maintain settings, drafts or locally generated records. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into features that do not explicitly require it.

7. Website Usage

Users are expected to use the website responsibly. Attempts to damage, abuse, manipulate or interfere with website functionality are not permitted.

8. Changes to Policy

ARS Official may update these policies when platform features or requirements change. The latest version published on this page should be considered the current website policy.
