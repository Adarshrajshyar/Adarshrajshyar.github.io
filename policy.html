ARS Policies

Please read the following guidelines before using ARS services and features.

🌹 About ARS

ARS is the official creative platform of Adarsh Raj, featuring Shayari, stories, poetry, creative content and ARS community activities.

📜 Content Policy

Users and publishers should submit original, respectful and appropriate content.

Content that is abusive, hateful, misleading, illegal or harmful may be removed.

✍️ Author & Credit Policy

Every published Shayari, story or poem may display the name of its author.

Do not claim another person's work as your own. Proper credit should be provided wherever applicable.

🏅 Certificate Policy

ARS certificates are generated for the purpose specified on the certificate.

Normal certificates may be generated through the certificate section by entering the required information.

ARS Joining / Membership certificates require approval of the submitted joining request before they become available.

🔎 Certificate Verification

Each ARS certificate should contain a unique certificate ID that can be checked through the ARS Verify page.

If certificate information does not match the verification record, it should not be treated as an authentic ARS certificate.

🤝 Joining Policy

Joining requests are reviewed by ARS. Approval is required before an official ARS Membership / Joining Certificate is issued.

Please provide accurate information while submitting a joining request.

🔐 Admin & Publisher

Admin and Publisher panels are intended only for authorized ARS management.

Login credentials should not be shared publicly.

📩 Contact Policy

Contact messages should be respectful and relevant to ARS.

Spam, repeated unwanted messages and abusive communication may be ignored or removed.

❤️ Like & Favorite

Like and Favorite features are provided for interacting with ARS content.

These features do not represent official endorsements or awards.

🌐 Website Usage

By using the ARS website, visitors agree to use its features responsibly and respectfully.

ARS may update its website, content, features or policies when necessary.
