Privacy Policy

ARS — Adarsh Ke Alfaz

1. Introduction

ARS — Adarsh Ke Alfaz respects the privacy of its visitors, students, members, contributors and other users. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when using the ARS website and related services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the service used, ARS may collect information such as:

  • Name and profile information
  • Email address
  • Registered mobile number
  • Educational/class information
  • Exam registration and attempt information
  • Result and certificate information
  • Join ARS application information
  • Messages submitted through contact forms

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide website services, process exam registrations, manage student accounts, generate eligible certificates, process Join ARS applications, provide notifications, respond to enquiries and maintain website security.

4. Examination Data

Examination systems may process registration details, answers, marks, attempt information, timing data and result records for examination administration and evaluation.

Examination integrity features should collect only information necessary for the announced purpose and must be implemented with appropriate notice and consent where required.

5. Cookies and Analytics

ARS may use cookies, local storage or analytics technologies for essential functionality, session management, performance measurement and website improvement, subject to the applicable configuration and legal requirements.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, misuse or disclosure. Authentication credentials must be handled by a secure backend and should not be hardcoded in public website files.

7. Third-Party Services

ARS may use trusted third-party services for authentication, database hosting, email, analytics, document generation, storage or other website functionality. Such services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Certificates and Verification

Certificate records may contain information required to verify certificate authenticity. Public verification pages should expose only the minimum information necessary for legitimate verification.

9. Children's and Student Data

ARS may provide educational services intended for school students. Where users are minors, appropriate parent/guardian involvement, consent and applicable legal requirements should be followed before collecting or processing information.

10. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, operational requirements, security, legal obligations or legitimate record-keeping needs.

11. Your Choices and Requests

Users may contact ARS regarding applicable questions about their information, corrections, access or other privacy-related requests, subject to verification and applicable requirements.

12. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when ARS services, technology, legal requirements or operational practices change. The latest version should be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use the official ARS Contact page.
