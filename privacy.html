Privacy and trust

Privacy Notice Draft

Finalise and review this notice before collecting any user information.

Data minimisation

Collect only the information needed to operate a clearly described feature. Explain why it is needed, who can access it, how long it is kept and how a person can ask a question or request correction/deletion where applicable.

Student accounts

Because the platform may serve minors, use age-appropriate notices and obtain any required parent/guardian consent before collecting or processing children’s personal data.

Security

Do not store passwords in plain text or in frontend code. Use a reputable authentication provider, least-privilege access, secure server-side checks and appropriate audit logging.

Contact and retention

Add the verified operator/contact details, retention period, grievance process, third-party services and applicable jurisdiction before launch.

This is a planning draft, not legal advice or a final privacy policy.
