Exam Rules

Draft rules to finalise before any real exam is opened.

  1. Read the official ARS notice for the final date, time, eligibility and syllabus.
  2. Use only one authorised registration/account per participant, according to final rules.
  3. Do not impersonate another candidate or share exam credentials.
  4. Follow the allowed-resource and attempt-time instructions.
  5. Submit before the timer ends; the final system must clearly explain how autosave and submission work.
  6. Report access or accessibility issues through the published contact route.
  7. Results and certificates are official only after the approved review and publication workflow.
  8. Dispute, correction and privacy processes must be published before registration opens.

These are draft principles, not final enforceable exam terms. Admin approval and legal/privacy review are required before a real examination.
