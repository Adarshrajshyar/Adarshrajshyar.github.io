ARS Examination Rules

General instructions for participants of ARS online examinations.

Important Notice

Every ARS examination may have examination-specific rules. Participants must read the rules, instructions, eligibility requirements and schedule published for the particular examination before starting.

Where an examination-specific rule conflicts with a general rule shown here, the officially published examination-specific rule should be followed.

Registration Rules

  1. Participants must register within the announced registration period.
  2. Registration information should be entered accurately.
  3. A registration ID should be kept safely for future reference.
  4. Participants should check their registered contact information before the examination.

Before Starting the Exam

  • Log in using the authorized account or exam access method.
  • Keep a stable internet connection.
  • Use a supported and suitable device.
  • Read all instructions shown before beginning.
  • Enter the examination only after confirming the displayed exam details.

During the Examination

  • Only one attempt should be permitted where the examination configuration specifies one attempt.
  • Questions may be randomized from the authorized question pool.
  • Options may also be randomized.
  • Participants may use Previous, Next and Mark for Review where enabled.
  • Answer changes are permitted where the exam configuration allows them.
  • The examination timer must be followed.
  • The system may automatically submit the attempt when the timer expires.

Examination Integrity

Participants must follow all instructions intended to maintain a fair examination environment.

  • Do not impersonate another participant.
  • Do not attempt unauthorized access to the examination system.
  • Do not intentionally interfere with the functioning of the examination platform.
  • Do not use prohibited assistance where such assistance is expressly disallowed.
  • Follow any full-screen or navigation controls displayed by the examination system.
Important: Technical or integrity-related events may be recorded by the examination system when the relevant feature is enabled and properly disclosed.

Suspicious Activity Review

Where an exam includes suspicious-activity detection, relevant system events may be flagged for authorized review. A flag does not automatically mean that a participant has violated a rule; appropriate review should be completed before a final decision.

Evaluation and Results

  1. Submitted answers are evaluated according to the examination configuration.
  2. A provisional evaluation may be generated automatically.
  3. Authorized personnel may review the result where required.
  4. The final result is published only through the approved result workflow.

Qualification & Merit

Qualification criteria and merit criteria must be announced for the relevant examination before they are applied to participants.

Participation, Achievement and Merit recognition should not be assumed unless the participant meets the published criteria.

Certificate Rules

  • Participation Certificate may be issued to eligible participants according to the published certificate policy.
  • Achievement Certificate depends on the announced qualification criteria.
  • Merit recognition depends on the published merit criteria.
  • Certificate records may contain a unique certificate ID and QR-based verification.

Technical Issues

In case of a technical interruption, participants should use the official ARS support/contact channel and provide the relevant registration or attempt information when requested.

The final handling of a technical incident depends on the examination configuration and available system records.

Final Responsibility

Participants are responsible for reading the latest examination instructions before starting the exam. ARS may update examination instructions before an examination begins in accordance with the published rules and applicable requirements.
