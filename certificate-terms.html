Certificate Terms

ARS — Adarsh Ke Alfaz

1. About ARS Certificates

ARS may issue digital certificates for eligible examinations, achievements, participation, merit recognition and approved ARS membership/joining activities.

2. Certificate Categories

  • Joining Certificate
  • Participation Certificate
  • Achievement Certificate
  • Merit Recognition / Merit Certificate
Joining Certificates are separate from certificates associated with examination participation or achievement.

3. Certificate Eligibility

A certificate will be issued only when the participant satisfies the applicable eligibility conditions and the certificate has been officially approved or generated through the ARS system.

Qualification and merit thresholds must be announced for the applicable examination before they are used to determine eligibility.

4. Certificate Information

Depending on the certificate type, a certificate may include information such as participant name, certificate title, certificate ID, examination or membership information, date, authorized signature and QR verification information.

5. QR Verification

ARS certificates may contain a QR code linked to an official verification page. The verification page should allow a person to check the certificate record using the unique certificate ID or other approved verification information.

6. Certificate Status

Valid Officially issued and currently valid
Revoked Withdrawn after issuance
Cancelled Certificate cancelled by authorized process
Not Found No matching official record

7. Correction of Certificate Records

Where an official certificate contains a genuine data or administrative error, the holder may contact ARS through the official support channel. Corrections should be handled through an authorized record management process.

8. Revocation or Cancellation

A certificate may be revoked or cancelled where the underlying record is determined to be invalid, incorrectly issued, fraudulently obtained or otherwise subject to authorized administrative action.

Certificate status changes should be recorded through the authorized certificate management and audit process.

9. Digital Certificate

Digital certificates are provided electronically and may be made available for download through the authenticated student account or an approved certificate access process.

10. Authenticity

A certificate should be considered authentic only when the information can be verified through the official ARS verification system.

11. Contact for Verification Issues

Questions concerning certificate records or verification should be submitted through the official ARS Contact page.

12. Policy Updates

These Certificate Terms may be updated when the ARS certificate system, verification process or administrative requirements change.
