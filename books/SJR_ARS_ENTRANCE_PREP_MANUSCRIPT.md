## Study method
1. Learn the idea in a short note.
2. Work through examples without looking at the solution.
3. Solve a small practice set.
4. Record incorrect answers and the reason for each mistake.
5. Revisit errors after one day, one week and one month.
6. Take timed practice only after understanding the concepts.

## Mathematics starter note — Factors and multiples
A **factor** divides a number exactly. A **multiple** is the result of multiplying a number by a whole number. For example, 3 is a factor of 12 because 12 ÷ 3 = 4, and 12 is a multiple of 3 because 3 × 4 = 12.

**Practice:** List all factors of 18. Then write the first five positive multiples of 4.

## Reasoning starter note — Number patterns
Look for a consistent difference, multiplication rule, alternating sequence or another rule that explains every shown term. Check the proposed rule against the full pattern rather than guessing from only two numbers.

**Practice:** 5, 8, 11, 14, __. Describe the rule before selecting the next term.

## Reading starter routine
- Read the full passage once for its main idea.
- Read the question and identify exactly what it asks.
- Return to the passage to find evidence.
- Eliminate options that add information not supported by the passage.

## Separate exam tracks
### Sainik School / AISSEE
Create a class/year-specific syllabus checklist after checking the current official AISSEE notice. Keep a dedicated section for the target entry class, subject distribution, exam language/options and application requirements only after verified.

### JNV / Navodaya
Use the latest JNV admission notice to confirm the target class, eligibility and paper structure. Keep mental ability, arithmetic and language practice organised according to the published pattern.

### RMS
Consult the current Rashtriya Military Schools admission notification and organise practice according to the target entry class, eligibility and prescribed subjects.
