Apply to become part of the ARS learner and contributor community.

How the application is planned to work

  1. Complete the application and confirm consent.
  2. Receive an application reference after secure submission is connected.
  3. The authorised team reviews the application.
  4. The applicant sees Pending, Approved or Rejected status.
  5. Approved members may receive a membership ID and joining certificate.

The form on this preview does not send or save applications. Do not submit sensitive or private information here.
